# Nine Minds Podcast Distillation Notes

This roundtable is a fictional educational dialogue. The goal is historically grounded style distillation, not literal quotation, voice cloning, or deceptive impersonation.

## Distillation Method

1. Use public biographies, lectures, primary-adjacent sources, and reputable cultural institutions.
2. Reduce each figure to a compact "operating principle": what they repeatedly cared about, how they tended to reason, and what tension they bring to the central question.
3. Write new dialogue in a contemporary podcast style while preserving those principles.
4. Keep the audio as synthetic cast performance. Do not imitate a known real voice.

## Figure Notes

### Albert Einstein

- Sources: Nobel Prize facts, Einstein's public writing on science/religion, and later public responsibility debates around science.
- Distilled principle: distrust the obvious; frames matter; wonder should make expertise humble; scientists cannot entirely separate equations from public consequence.
- Useful dialogue traits: reflective, aphoristic, wary of narrow professional ownership.
- Sources:
  - https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1921/einstein/facts/
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/146605a0
  - https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00963402.1952.11457266

### Marie Curie

- Sources: Nobel Prize biography and discovery account; AIP Curie archive excerpts.
- Distilled principle: disciplined measurement makes invisible forces public; patience outranks glamour; useful science comes through repeated labor.
- Useful dialogue traits: spare, exact, empirical, resistant to fame-first framing.
- Sources:
  - https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1903/marie-curie/article/
  - https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/themes/marie-and-pierre-curie-and-the-discovery-of-polonium-and-radium/
  - https://history.aip.org/history/exhibits/curie/brief/06_quotes/quotes_07.html

### Richard Feynman

- Sources: Nobel Prize facts/biography and Caltech's transcript of "Cargo Cult Science."
- Distilled principle: joy in discovery must be paired with aggressive self-suspicion; nature judges theories; integrity means reporting what could prove you wrong.
- Useful dialogue traits: lively, concrete, skeptical of ceremony, allergic to fake certainty.
- Sources:
  - https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1965/feynman/facts/
  - https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1965/feynman/biographical/
  - https://calteches.library.caltech.edu/51/2/CargoCult.htm

### Socrates

- Sources: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy and Plato's Apology context.
- Distilled principle: examine the knower before celebrating knowledge; wisdom begins by exposing false certainty; ethics must precede cleverness.
- Useful dialogue traits: cross-examining, ironic, question-led, focused on the good life.
- Sources:
  - https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/socrates/
  - https://www.britannica.com/topic/Apology-by-Plato

### Confucius

- Sources: Analects-focused summaries and Stanford/Britannica-adjacent ethical framing.
- Distilled principle: knowledge becomes humane through relationships, roles, ritual practice, duty, and cultivated conduct.
- Useful dialogue traits: teacherly, balanced, relational, suspicious of cleverness without character.
- Sources:
  - https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2024/entries/ethics-chinese/
  - https://philopedia.org/works/the-analects-of-confucius/

### William Shakespeare

- Sources: Folger Shakespeare Library pages on major plays and Britannica on Shakespeare's works.
- Distilled principle: knowledge passes through motive; ambition, jealousy, grief, love, and power can bend what people know.
- Useful dialogue traits: theatrical, image-rich, attuned to irony and tragic cost.
- Sources:
  - https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-works/king-lear/about-shakespeares-king-lear/
  - https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-works/macbeth/macbeth-a-modern-perspective
  - https://www.britannica.com/art/English-literature/Shakespeares-works

### Leonardo da Vinci

- Sources: Metropolitan Museum of Art and Britannica.
- Distilled principle: drawing is investigation; art and science meet in observation, mechanics, anatomy, water, flight, and repeated looking.
- Useful dialogue traits: visual, tactile, design-minded, experimental.
- Sources:
  - https://www.metmuseum.org/en/essays/leonardo-da-vinci-1452-1519
  - https://www.britannica.com/biography/Leonardo-da-Vinci

### Frida Kahlo

- Sources: Frida Kahlo Museum biography, Philadelphia Museum of Art, and curated biographical material.
- Distilled principle: truth returns to the body; pain, identity, politics, color, and private experience are not abstractions.
- Useful dialogue traits: intimate, vivid, unsentimental, embodied.
- Sources:
  - https://www.museofridakahlo.org.mx/wp/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Biografias-Frida-Kahlo-ingles.pdf
  - https://www.philamuseum.org/exhibitions/frida-kahlo
  - https://www.fridakahlo.org/

### Nelson Mandela

- Sources: Nobel Prize biography/article and Nelson Mandela Foundation democracy page.
- Distilled principle: justice must become durable institutions; reconciliation is active political construction, not sentimental forgiveness.
- Useful dialogue traits: deliberate, public-minded, dignity-centered, repair-oriented.
- Sources:
  - https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/1993/mandela/
  - https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/1993/mandela/biographical/
  - https://www.nelsonmandela.org/democracy

## Audio Direction

OpenAI TTS should use distinct built-in voices per speaker with instructions for temperament only. Avoid instructions that request an exact real-world voice match.

## Animation Direction

The local Remotion renderer can produce controlled editorial animation: camera moves, lighting, stage blocking, subtitles, and data-driven speaker timing. True SOTA generated video clips such as Sora/Runway/Veo/Luma-style shots require a connected video-generation API and should use non-deceptive stylized historical archetypes rather than exact photoreal public-figure impersonations.
