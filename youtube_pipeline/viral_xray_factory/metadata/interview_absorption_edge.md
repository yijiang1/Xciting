## Voiceover Script
1. Welcome back. Today our guest is an atom, and the question is personal: why do you absorb X-rays?
2. The atom says: I only take energy packets that can kick an inner electron into an allowed state.
3. Below the edge, the photon is too weak. It knocks, but nobody answers.
4. At the edge, the photon has enough energy to launch a core electron. The absorption jumps.
5. Near the edge, the outgoing electron wave feels the local chemistry and shape of empty states.
6. That is why XAS can tell oxidation state, coordination, and local structure without asking the crystal to behave.
